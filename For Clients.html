FAQ

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

If your situation isn't covered here, we'd rather have a 30-minute call than send a proposal blind. Reach out — we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

What kind of expertise do you cover?
AI and cybersecurity are our two practice areas. Around them we keep deep benches in data, platform and software engineering, plus independent senior architects. If your problem is in those zones and you need a recognised specialist, we can help.
How do you choose the consultants in your network?
A partner intro call, two technical references we actually call, and proof of shipped specialist work — papers, talks, CVEs, production systems they led. We stay small on purpose; the bar is what makes the network valuable to clients.
How do you price?
Specialist time-and-materials with a clear scope and ceiling is our default. Fixed price for well-defined deliverables. Either way, you see the consultant's rate, our margin, and what the work actually costs. No tier games.
Do you work on-site? Cleared environments?
Yes — we expect to be on-site for at least part of every engagement. A meaningful share of our cybersecurity and AI work is in regulated, sensitive or cleared environments; background checks, NDAs and signed access agreements are routine.
Who owns the code, models and findings?
You do, always. Source, infra-as-code, model artefacts, evaluation suites, security findings and reports — delivered into your repos and your accounts as part of the engagement, not at the end.
Will I get the named specialist, or someone behind them?
The named one. Profiles are real; we don't run a pyramid. If the consultant you met in the pitch can't continue for any reason, we tell you immediately and you choose the replacement — not us.
