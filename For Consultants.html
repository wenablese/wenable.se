FAQ

Common questions from people thinking about applying.

If your situation isn't here, send a note — we'd rather have a 20-minute call than miss someone who'd be a great fit.

Do I need to be Gothenburg-based?
No, but most of our active assignments are in the Nordics with at least some on-site time expected. If you're EU-based and travel-flexible, that works. We're upfront about location requirements before any conversation goes anywhere.
Do I have to be a registered AB / firma?
Yes — we contract consultant-to-consultancy, not as employees. AB or enskild firma in Sweden, the equivalent in your country. We can point you at accountants if you're mid-setup.
What does "specialist-grade rate" actually mean?
Rates anchored to what you'd charge a direct client for the same depth — not a generic consulting band with our margin layered on top. You see the client rate and our margin on every assignment. The margin is fixed. The rate isn't.
How exclusive is the network?
Non-exclusive. We don't ask you to turn down direct clients or other networks. What we do ask: tell us when you're booked elsewhere, and don't double-book us on assignments we've started conversations on.
How often do assignments come up?
For active practice areas (AI, cybersecurity), typically a few new conversations per consultant per quarter. We deliberately don't try to keep you fully booked — we'd rather over-curate than burn out the network.
What if my speciality isn't AI or cyber?
We also work with senior data, platform and software engineers, plus independent architects, around the AI and cyber practices. Apply anyway and we'll be honest about whether we can place you well.
